Key Insights
📱

Mobile Experience is Critical

45% access LMS frequently/always on mobile, yet “Poor mobile app functionality” is the 3rd biggest challenge (28 mentions).

😕

Confusing UI is #1 Pain Point

51 students (43%) reported confusing user interface — the most frequent complaint by a clear margin.

✅

Core Features Are Valued

Access to lecture notes (56) and Assignment Submission (50) dominate useful features. Video lectures lag behind.

🔀

Fragmented Tool Ecosystem

Teachers heavily use Telegram (56), Google Classroom (54) & WhatsApp (50) alongside formal LMS platforms.

⚠️

Reliability Concerns Exist

Technical glitches rank #2 (44 mentions). Only ~55% are Satisfied or Very Satisfied overall.

🎓

Science-Heavy Sample

78% of respondents are from Science (B.Sc/IT/CS/Biotech). Findings best reflect that cohort.

Recommended Improvements (Mobile-First Focus)
  • Simplify navigation — Reduce clicks to reach notes, assignments and grades. Students repeatedly asked for a cleaner, more intuitive interface.
  • Prioritize mobile app quality — Fix crashes, improve performance and make the mobile experience first-class. Nearly half of users rely on mobile frequently.
  • Improve reliability & uptime — Address technical glitches and system crashes that undermine trust in the platform.
  • Timely updates & notifications — Students want faster content updates and better alert systems for deadlines and new materials.
  • Integrate popular channels — Since Telegram, WhatsApp and Google Classroom are already heavily used, explore better bridging or single-source workflows.
  • Enhance core workflows — Double down on easy assignment submission, quick note access and clear grade tracking — the features students value most.
